# Passport-To-The-Star
In this world, everyone has a story to tell. NPCs will provide you with information, testimonies, and clues, but not everything is as it seems. Your job is to be a sharp-eyed verifier, carefully cross-checking every detail they provide to find the contradictions and mismatches. Can you spot the hidden truth buried beneath the lies?
